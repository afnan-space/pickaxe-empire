Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We keep only what the game needs to work, stay fair and pay you. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

What we store

DataWhy
Your Telegram user ID, name, @username, profile photo link, language and whether you have Telegram Premium (sent by Telegram when you open the app)Your account, your profile and the leaderboard
Your game progress: balances, level, mining, check-ins, tasks, invites, and a log of balance changesRunning the game
The TON wallet address you connect and its public keyCounting your on-chain PXE and sending your withdrawals
Withdrawal requests and their statusPaying you, and showing your history
A random install ID kept by the app on your phone, the Telegram app and browser version, and a one-way hash of your network address (the address itself is not stored)Detecting multiple accounts and bots, so rewards go to real players
Your country, worked out from your network address (only the two-letter country code is kept)Showing you the tasks and partner offers available where you are
When you last used the appActivity stats and anti-bot checks

Public blockchain data

To check holdings and payouts we read public TON blockchain data about wallets connected to the game, such as balances, transfers and when a wallet was first used.

Who processes it

  • Telegram delivers the app and our bot messages.
  • Railway hosts our server and database; Cloudflare hosts the app and this website.
  • ipwho.is and country.is tell us which country a network address is in; they receive the address, nothing else.
  • TonAPI and Toncenter provide blockchain data and send payouts on TON.

Messages from the bot

The bot can message you about friends joining, withdrawals and game news, if you started the bot or allowed messages in the app. You can block the bot at any time.

Partners

Partner tasks may ask a partner app whether you completed something there, using your Telegram ID. Partners we work with can ask us whether a player met a requirement; the answer is yes or no with progress numbers, never your personal details.

Keeping and deleting

We keep account data while your account exists, and records of payouts as long as we need them to prevent fraud and settle disputes. To ask what we hold or to delete your account, contact us through the support link in the app.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
